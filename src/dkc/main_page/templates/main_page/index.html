The Legacy of Excellence

What it means to be a Distinguished Key Clubber

The Distinguished Key Clubber Award is one of the highest individual honors presented by the New York District. It celebrates members who consistently go above and beyond the call of duty—showing exemplary initiative, inspiring their peers, and embodying leadership in service.

Leadership

Demonstrating character, initiative, and responsibility within your club, division, and community.

Service

Unwavering dedication to hands-on service hours, Kiwanis Family projects, and global initiatives.

Advocacy

Actively spreading awareness for major causes, fundraising, and promoting Key Club's core values.

Excellence

Maintaining active membership in good standing while striving for quality in every statement and report.

Eligibility & Steps

Your pathway to earning the Distinguished Key Clubber Award

Who May Apply?

Any dues-paying Key Club member in good standing within the New York District is eligible to apply for this prestigious honor.

1
40% Weight

Personal Statement

A thoughtful essay highlighting your personal journey, leadership development, and commitment to Key Club.

2
20% Weight

Service Projects

Detailed reporting of hands-on service, Kiwanis Family events, Major Emphasis partners, and local causes.

3
20% Weight

Advocacy & Roles

Evidence of project advocacy, divisional/district event attendance, and leadership positions held.

4
20% Weight

Recommendations

A written endorsement from a Key Club officer, faculty advisor, or Kiwanis advisor in good standing.
